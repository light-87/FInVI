## About Your Idea

**Q: What are you building?**
> "Vivy lets anyone create AI trading strategies using natural language. Instead of writing Python code, you write: 'Buy tech stocks when their PE ratio drops below average after positive earnings.' Our AI reads real market news, analyzes it against your strategy, and gives you a trade recommendation with full reasoning - not a black box. Users compete on a public leaderboard. We're crowdsourcing 'semantic alpha' - trading strategies based on reasoning."

**Q: What's the problem you're solving?**
> "Algorithmic trading is a $14 billion market, but it's locked behind programming skills. You need Python, R, or C++ to participate. That means 99% of people with market insights can't act on them. Great ideas, no way to execute. We remove that barrier."

**Q: Why will this work?**
> "Three reasons:
> 1. AI is finally good enough - LLMs can now analyze earnings calls, news, and sentiment at PhD-level
> 2. Timing - 'Agentic AI' is the trend of 2025, and trading is a perfect use case for AI agents that take action
> 3. Market demand - Post-2020, millions of new retail traders have opinions but current tools still require code"

**Q: How do you make money?**
> "Three revenue streams:
> 1. **B2C Subscriptions** - Free tier, $19/mo Pro, $49/mo Power
> 2. **Sponsored Challenges** - Hedge funds pay $5K-50K to sponsor competitions and find alpha
> 3. **Strategy Licensing** - We license top-performing strategies to institutions, 70/30 revenue share with creators"

**Q: What's your unfair advantage / moat?**
> "Network effects. More users means a better leaderboard, which means more data. We're building a unique 'semantic alpha' dataset - trading strategies based on reasoning and language. No one else has this. Also, explainability is built-in, which is a regulatory advantage as AI transparency requirements increase."

# WHAT TO ASK THEM

At the end, they'll ask "Do you have questions?" Have 2-3 ready:

1. **"What does the first few weeks of the cohort look like? How do you facilitate co-founder matching?"**
   - Shows you're thinking practically about the program

2. **"What do the most successful EF founders have in common that surprised you?"**
   - Shows you want to learn and improve

3. **"Given what I've told you about Vivy, what's the biggest risk you see?"**
   - Shows intellectual honesty and openness to feedback

# THINGS TO AVOID

**DON'T:**
- Undersell yourself or be overly humble
- Say "I think" or "I hope" - be confident ("I will", "I am")
- Get too deep into technical details unless asked
- Talk for more than 60-90 seconds per answer
- Bad-mouth competitors
- Pretend you know things you don't

**DO:**
- Be specific with examples
- Show enthusiasm but stay grounded
- Admit what you need help with (that's why you want EF)
- Mention you have a WORKING product multiple times
- Be honest if you don't know something
